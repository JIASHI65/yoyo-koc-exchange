❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is this link safe? Is it a scam?
A: This is the official YoYo Town Creator Portal, hosted on GitHub Pages (a trusted platform). We never ask for your game password, credit cards, or sensitive documents.
Q: I've used my 2 submissions. Can I submit more?
A: Regular submissions are limited to 2/month. However, you can submit unlimited Showcase posts, and the limit resets next month.
Q: When will I receive my points/rewards?
A: Points: Admin reviews throughout the month. Digital rewards: Usually within a few days. Physical items: 2-4 weeks shipping.
Q: Game UID vs System ID - what's the difference?
A: Game UID: Your in-game username (has letters, like "YOYO-123") - used to login. System ID: 19-digit number - only needed for registration.
Q: I forgot my password. How do I reset it?
A: The portal doesn't use passwords! Just login with your Game UID each time. If you can't access your account, contact YoYo assistant💗Mochi on Discord.
Q: What is the CC (Continuous Creation) Reward?
A: If you submit 15+ works in a month, you automatically qualify for a bonus CC reward: Premium Monthly Card + Shelly Coins x500 + Gold x1000!
