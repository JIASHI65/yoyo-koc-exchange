Click "Submit New Work" and fill in:

🎁 Redeem Rewards

When the redemption window opens (admin announces in Discord):

  • Click "Redeem Rewards" on the homepage
  • Browse available rewards in 3 categories:
    • 💎 Diamonds - In-game currency
    • 🎮 Google Play Cards - Digital gift cards
    • 🧸 Merchandise - Physical items (requires shipping address)

  • Points are deducted immediately

Check order status: Click "My Orders" to see redemption history and shipping status.
